Raz Marshanski

About Me

I recently completed my M.Sc. in Computer Science at Ben-Gurion University of the Negev, advised by Prof. Omri Azencot.

My research interests lie at the intersection of representation learning, generative modeling, and dynamical systems for sequential data, with a focus on time-series forecasting. I’m particularly interested in modeling how complex systems evolve over time and in scaling such models to real-world applications.

I’m interested in research and applied scientist roles, including quantitative research, as well as research collaborations.
