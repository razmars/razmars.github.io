About Me

I'm a researcher at Ben-Gurion University of the Negev, working with Prof. Omri Azencot. My research lies at the intersection of generative modeling and dynamical systems, with a focus on probabilistic time-series forecasting.

I use Koopman operator theory to build structured and efficient generative models. My recent work, KGO, explores single-step generation through closed-form latent dynamics. I also work on efficient pretrained forecasting models, including Super-Linear.
