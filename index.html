Researcher · Ben-Gurion University of the Negev

I'm a researcher at Ben-Gurion University of the Negev, working with Prof. Omri Azencot. My research lies at the intersection of generative modeling and dynamical systems. I use Koopman operator theory to build generative models that are both structured and fast. My recent work, the Koopman Generative Operator (KGO, NeurIPS 2026), enables single-step flow-matching generation for probabilistic time-series forecasting.
