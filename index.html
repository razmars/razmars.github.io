Raz Marshanski

Researcher · Ben-Gurion University of the Negev

I'm a researcher at Ben-Gurion University of the Negev, working with Prof. Omri Azencot. My research lies at the intersection of generative modeling and dynamical systems: I use Koopman operator theory to build generative models that are both structured and fast.

My recent work, the Koopman Generative Operator (NeurIPS 2026), enables single-step flow-matching generation for probabilistic time-series forecasting.

NeurIPS 2026

Koopman Generative Operators for Efficient Probabilistic Time-Series Forecasting

Raz Marshanski, Liran Nochumsohn, Mayank Jauhari, Boris N. Oreshkin, Omri Azencot

KGO makes flow-matching generation single-step through a closed-form matrix exponential in a Koopman latent space. It is state of the art on most ProbTS benchmarks and at least 25× faster at inference than iterative generative models.
